TELEPHOTE 0.5.0 · AUDIO PREVIEW

Your iPhone.
Your microphone.

Optional phone audio, with mute, level controls and separate WAV recording.

Requires desktop 0.5.0 and iPhone companion 1.0 build 7. The public iPhone download is coming soon; older companions continue to support video. Phone audio and native meeting-app capture still need physical-device validation.

Enable phone audio

  1. Enable the microphone option in the iPhone companion and allow microphone access.
  2. Connect your camera by USB or local Wi-Fi.
  3. Open the desktop microphone tab and enable device audio. Use its level meter, mute and gain controls.
  4. Choose WAV recording or an available output. Select the microphone separately in your meeting app.

Windows

The portable app can receive phone audio, record WAV files and play through an existing audio output. A meeting app can receive that output only when a compatible audio bridge is already present.

A built-in, no-install USB microphone route is in development. It is not included in 0.5.0. The connected iPhone advertised a standard USB audio interface, but activation and native Teams microphone capture have not yet been verified. Telephote installs no audio driver.

Linux

Enable Linux microphone output, then select Telephote_Microphone in your meeting app. This creates a temporary source in an existing PulseAudio or PipeWire audio server using pactl. The server must support module-pipe-source. Telephote removes the source on normal shutdown and does not change persistent audio configuration.

Native Linux source enumeration, real phone speech and a live meeting are still awaiting verification. WAV recording does not require the temporary microphone source.

Recordings and privacy

Audio is 48 kHz mono. Microphone recordings are separate WAV files; MP4/MKV recordings contain video only. Microphone access is off by default and requires both phone and desktop opt-in. Stop ends the stream. Desktop mute and privacy pause silence the received audio. Read the app privacy policy.

What has been checked?

Automated checks cover audio packet validation, opt-in negotiation, video/audio multiplexing, gain, mute, bounded buffering and WAV output. Windows UI checks cover microphone controls and privacy behaviour. Generated test samples are not a successful iPhone or Teams test.
