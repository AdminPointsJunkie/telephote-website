CONNECT YOUR IPHONE

A cable.
Or a QR code.

Keep it wired at your desk, or give your camera a little more freedom on the same local network.

iPhoneUSB cableWindows PC

Plug in. Stay connected.

  1. Connect and unlock your iPhone. Complete any phone approval while Telephote prepares USB on Windows.
  2. Start the camera in the iPhone app. Enter its six-digit code in the Windows app and press Start camera.
  3. Choose AvStream Media Device in your meeting app’s camera settings.

USB uses the Apple USB hardware driver already present on compatible PCs. No Apple Devices app or service is installed by Telephote.

iPhoneSame local networkWindows PC

Scan once. Skip the typing.

  1. Connect the iPhone and PC to the same local network. The PC can use Wi-Fi or Ethernet.
  2. Choose Wi-Fi → Show QR code on Windows, then scan it inside the Telephote iPhone app.
  3. Keep both apps open, then select the camera output in your meeting app.

No links to copy. Wi-Fi pairing uses an encrypted local connection. Guest networks that isolate devices may prevent pairing.

A FEW USEFUL DETAILS

Before your
first call.

How much does Telephote cost?

The Telephote iPhone app costs A$5 (Australian dollars), paid once. The portable Windows app is free. There are no Telephote subscriptions or recurring fees. Both apps are needed for an iPhone webcam; the public iPhone download is coming soon. Third-party Android apps may charge separately.

Can I use an Android phone as a webcam?

The separate Android test build accepts a phone’s built-in USB Webcam mode or a local MJPEG feed from an Android webcam app, such as IP Webcam or DroidCam Classic. USB Webcam mode needs no phone companion. Network streaming needs a compatible phone app. Physical Android testing is still in progress. Read the Android setup guide.

Can I update without downloading a ZIP each time?

Version 0.3.1 and the Android test build include Updates → Check for updates. Download a newer matching version inside the app, stop camera and recording, then choose Restart & update. Users of older versions need to extract the updater-enabled ZIP once. Read the update guide.

Does “portable” really mean no Windows installer?

Yes. Extract the complete ZIP and run Telephote.exe from its folder. The Windows runtime is included. The iPhone companion is a separate app that needs to be installed on your phone.

Can I use it with Microsoft Teams?

Yes, Telephote makes its output available through Windows’ existing camera facilities. Enable “Use in Teams and other apps”, keep Telephote open and choose AvStream Media Device in Teams’ camera settings. Camera availability on a managed work PC still follows your organisation’s policies.

Do my iPhone and PC need the same Wi-Fi?

Only for Wi-Fi pairing. Both devices must be on the same local network; your PC can also be connected by Ethernet. A direct USB connection does not depend on the phone and PC sharing Wi-Fi.

What if USB isn’t available on my PC?

USB needs a compatible Apple USB hardware driver already on the PC. Telephote doesn’t install one. You can use local Wi-Fi pairing instead when both devices can reach each other on the same network.

Does Telephote send video to the cloud?

Telephote sends camera video directly to your PC over USB or an encrypted local Wi-Fi connection. Your meeting app handles the online call. Local recordings are saved where you choose on Windows.

Does it also replace my microphone?

Telephote carries video. Keep using your headset, laptop microphone or another microphone selected in your meeting app.
