Telephote.

Privacy policy

Effective 28 November 2026

Telephote sends camera video and, when you enable it in a compatible companion, microphone audio to the Windows or Linux computer you pair with. The developer does not receive, store or sell your camera feed, microphone audio or pairing codes. The iPhone app has no account, advertising or analytics.

Camera and local network

Camera access lets you preview video, stream it and scan a pairing code. Local network access lets Telephote reach your PC over Wi-Fi. Wi-Fi video uses an encrypted local connection. USB video travels through the cable. Tap Stop camera to end the session. You can change either permission in iPhone Settings.

Optional microphone

Microphone access is off by default. The build 7 companion requests iOS microphone permission only when you enable its microphone option. Audio streaming also requires the desktop microphone option. It uses the same direct USB or encrypted local Wi-Fi connection as video. Stop the camera session or turn off the phone microphone to stop capture. You can revoke microphone access in iPhone Settings.

Recordings and meeting apps

When you choose to record or take a snapshot in a desktop companion, the file is saved on your computer. Microphone recordings are separate WAV files; recorded MP4/MKV video files do not include an audio track. You control those files and can delete them there. Your meeting app handles the call and its own privacy settings.

Download statistics

Desktop download counts and optional download-request analytics are separate from the iPhone app. See download privacy for IP/location coverage and the one-year retention period.

Website and support

This website and the public support tracker are hosted by GitHub. If you post a support request, GitHub handles your account and the information you submit under its privacy statement. Support reports are public. Keep pairing codes, personal images and account details private.

For questions about this policy, visit Telephote support.
